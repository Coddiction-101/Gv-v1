# 1. What is Social Media Marketing (SMM)?

Social Media Marketing (SMM) is the use of social media platforms to promote products, services, or brands and engage with customers.

### Definition
"Social Media Marketing is a digital marketing strategy that uses social media platforms to build brand awareness, increase engagement, generate leads, and drive sales."

# 12. Important Metrics

## Reach

Number of unique users who see content.

---

## Impressions

Total number of times content is displayed.

Formula:

Reach ≠ Impressions

Example:

1 User views post 5 times

Reach = 1

Impressions = 5

---

## Engagement Rate

Measures audience interaction.

Formula:

Engagement Rate =
(Total Engagements ÷ Total Followers) × 100

---

## Click-Through Rate (CTR)

Percentage of users who click a link.

Formula:

CTR =
(Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100

---

## Conversion Rate

Percentage of users completing desired actions.

Formula:

Conversion Rate =
(Conversions ÷ Visitors) × 100
